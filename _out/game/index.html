ASHGATE WORKS: an original round-based tactical FPS in three.js. No third-party art, models, audio, names or map: every texture is painted on a canvas in materials.js, every mesh is built in code, and the yard, the rifle and the callouts (A SLAG BASIN / B KILN HOUSE) were authored for this entry. Modules, one job each: main.js (input, fixed-step simulation, render loop, ?shot= still harness), state.js (one mutable match state), config.js (weapon/ballistics/economy/palette tables), player.js (acceleration, crouch, step-up collision), weapon.js (hitscan from the muzzle, spread and recoil, headshot multiplier, FX pools), bots.js (humanoid bodies plus waypoint/sight/sound minds), rounds.js (buy phase, economy, win conditions, plant and defuse), hud.js (readouts bound to state), audio.js (WebAudio synthesis, no files), materials.js (procedural canvas textures), world.js (props over the playable brushes), lighting.js (low warm sun, sky dome, haze), post.js (bloom, ACES, grade). -->
